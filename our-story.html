How it started

In 1969, Jay Hippey and George Sirianni bought the old meat plant on Railroad Street in
Denver, Pennsylvania. They made ring bologna and franks the way their customers'
grandparents knew them: good beef and pork, tied by hand, smoked over hickory. They
sold them under the Hippey's name, and people in Lancaster County have been buying
them since.

The Hippey family's meat-making roots go back to 1969.
