HOW WE MAKE IT

Some smoked meat is smoked with a liquid. Ours is smoked with wood.

Our body comes from Dillsburg and our pork from Souderton.
We stuff it, tie every casing by hand, and smoke it over real hickory in
the same building we've used since 1969. It takes longer, and you can
taste the difference.
